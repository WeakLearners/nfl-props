Graded picks

Explore results

Every shortlisted leg from a finished game, cut the way you choose. Needed is the hit rate at which $100 on each leg breaks even. A group only paid if its hit rate beat it. $100 each is what a flat $100 on every leg in the group would have returned.

← All reports

A leg is graded once its game is final, by the same rule the listing uses: the real number beats the line. An anytime-TD pick hits on a rushing or receiving touchdown; passing TDs don't count. TD picks without a book price count toward hit rate only. Rows under ten graded legs are greyed out — at that size a hit rate is mostly luck. Prices are what the report held when it was generated. Opponent defense only groups legs the model actually used that number for — passing yards, completions, and rushing yards (rushing at half weight). It's never applied to receiving yards or receptions, so those legs sit outside this grouping rather than being counted against a number that didn't shape them.
