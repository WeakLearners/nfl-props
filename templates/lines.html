Spread, total and win chance

Game lines

Entertainment only. This model does not beat the closing line, and nothing here claims an edge. It is kept apart from the player props and the touchdown picks on purpose.

Predictions

Record against the closing line

Each week's predictions are frozen the first time they are made and are never rewritten by a later refit. The spread is the model's margin for the home team. A lean is the side the model prefers against the current line; it is a record, not a pick. Win chance comes from the margin and the model's own error spread. The market win chance is the moneyline with the book's margin removed.
