Badges are rare by design. Most players and defenses have none. Each badge has a hover note with the numbers behind it. Badges describe past games and one matchup. They are not picks.

▲▼Player trend. Production per game, opponents removed, over the last 3 games against the 6 to 12 before. It needs a large change (t of 2 or more, at least 25%) and the same direction in 2 of the 3 games.

▲▼Defense trend, by category. Up means the defense got tougher (it allows less). Down means it allows more. Same test on its last 3 games against the 8 to 14 before.
